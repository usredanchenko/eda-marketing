# Acme Focus — forbidden claims

- Any number of users, downloads, ratings or reviews (`acme-focus-user-count` is NEEDS_CONFIRMATION).
- "Scientifically proven", "doubles your productivity" or any outcome promise (`acme-focus-productivity-claims` is FORBIDDEN).
- Android or web availability (`acme-focus-android`).
- Sync, accounts or cloud features — they do not exist.
- Mockups of unreleased screens without an honesty label.
