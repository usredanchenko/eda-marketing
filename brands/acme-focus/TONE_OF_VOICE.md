# Acme Focus — tone of voice

## Voice
Calm, direct, a little self-ironic. Honest about the beta.

## Do
- Speak like a person at a desk, not a coach on a stage.
- Use concrete moments ("I opened the laptop and checked email for 40 minutes").

## Don't
- Hype, guru phrases, "hack", "10x", fear of missing out (`config/lang/en/banned-phrases.yaml`).

## Example lines
- Good: "I built a timer that doesn't care if you skipped yesterday."
- Bad: "Unlock your ultimate productivity potential!"
