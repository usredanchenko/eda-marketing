# Acme Focus — audience

## Primary audience
Freelancers, students and indie developers (20–35) who watch productivity and build-in-public content on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## Pains and desires
- Starting is the hardest part; to-do lists grow, nothing gets done.
- Productivity apps feel like games that punish missed days.

## What they ignore / dislike
- "10x your productivity" promises, guru tone, fake screenshots.
