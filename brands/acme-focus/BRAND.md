# Acme Focus — brand

> **Fictional example brand** shipped with eda-marketing so every template, test and the quick start work out of the box. Replace it with your own brand (`npm run mos -- brand new <id>`).

## What it is
Acme Focus is a small focus-timer app for people who work alone: one timer, one task, no streaks or leaderboards.

## Stage
Public beta on iOS. The Android version is in development and has no release date. The web version does not exist.

## Author on camera
Alex, the solo developer. Talks to camera from the desk where the app is built.

## Visual identity
Deep ink background, warm coral accent, Manrope + JetBrains Mono (`tokens.json`).
